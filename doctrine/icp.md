# Doctrine — Ideal Customer Profile (ICP)

**Status: UNRESOLVED (U-005). Nothing here is policy yet.**

Per the research base (`knowledge/market/`), ICP definition is the canonical first step
of the entire sales build — before outreach, tooling, or hiring (Predictable Revenue,
Dream 100, Roberge; 3-0 verified). It is deliberately **not** filled in by inference
(rule 3): it is the subject of **Interview #1** with Tom.

## Questions Interview #1 must answer

1. Rank the segments: café chains / bars / restaurants / bakeries / hotels / offices /
   retail — by contribution, fit, and joy-to-serve.
2. Who is the buyer we target: single-site owner vs. chain HQ / purchasing?
3. What makes a **dream** account (Dream-100 criteria): size, branches, menu fit,
   payment behavior, logistics fit?
4. What makes a **bad-fit** account we should decline or price differently?
5. Where does the margin actually come from (product families × segment)?

Output: ranked ICP + Dream-100 criteria, `authority: user_confirmed`, logged here.
