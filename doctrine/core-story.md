# Doctrine — Core Story (Education-Based Marketing)

**Status: UNRESOLVED. Nothing here is approved copy.**

The Core Story is the single education-first narrative (Chet Holmes) that teaches the
buyer something valuable about *their* business — e.g., the economics of a cocktail/
beverage program for a café or restaurant — and positions GT as the authority. One
story feeds every channel (site, email, samples kit, Dream-100 touches).

Raw material comes from **Interview #5** (+ GT margin/menu data where verifiable).
Hebrew customer-facing copy additionally requires Tom-approved register entries per
PRODUCTION locked decisions.
