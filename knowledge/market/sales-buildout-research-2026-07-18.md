# Verified research — building a B2B sales operation from scratch (GT-calibrated)

> Authority: `doc_confirmed` (cited sources; confidence per finding).
> Method: deep-research pass, 2026-07-18 — 5 search angles, 22 sources fetched,
> 104 claims extracted, top 25 adversarially verified (3 independent verifier votes
> each): **22 confirmed, 3 refuted, 0 unverified.** Refuted claims listed at the end —
> they are knowledge too.

## Findings (confidence · sources)

1. **ICP first — unanimously.** Define target market + Ideal Customer Profile before
   outreach, tooling, or hiring. Predictable Revenue's 10-step sequence, Holmes'
   Dream-100 7-step, Roberge's hiring formula all sequence profiling/list before
   collateral/launch. *(high · predictablerevenue.com [primary], predictableprofits.com,
   nateliason.com/Roberge)*
2. **Roberge build order:** Hiring → Training → Management/process → Demand Gen —
   systematize how you sell before scaling lead flow; **compensation is the lever for
   the #1 metric.** HubSpot case: comp stack-ranked by churn cut churn 70%/6mo
   (SaaS case — mechanism transfers, magnitude does not). *(high · Wiley ToC + Roberge
   HBR corroboration)*
3. **Founder-led first.** Owner personally runs and closes the first proactive cycles;
   hires scale a working motion, they don't create one. Ops starts fractional. Keep
   new-business prospecting time-boxed and separate from reorder firefighting.
   *(medium · closingfoundry.com, stage2.capital, predictablerevenue.com; SaaS-origin)*
4. **Chains = one key account** via an account hierarchy (parent→branch family tree
   rolling contacts/orders/opportunities up to HQ). KAM matures in stages (Davies &
   Ryals, Springer — primary). **Calibration: owner is the single account owner; the
   hierarchy is a data structure, not headcount** (tiered KAM teams are enterprise-scale
   over-engineering here). *(high · gtmnow + Salesforce docs, Springer, demandfarm)*
5. **Land-and-expand + white-space.** Land small (HQ or one branch) → prove value →
   expand across branches; map which products each branch has NOT bought. *(medium ·
   kapta, demandfarm, gtmnow; SaaS-origin analogy branches≈subsidiaries — flagged)*
6. **Expansion economics favor existing accounts** (cheaper than new logos, higher LTV;
   NRR levers: price, upsell, cross-sell, new locations). **Contested:** Ehrenberg-Bass
   (Byron Sharp) credibly argues acquisition drives most growth — and GT's stated
   bottleneck IS acquisition (~3–29 new/mo). Treat retention-first as a priority
   weighting, not a settled law. *(medium; economics vote was 2-1)*
7. **Wholesale churn is silent** — accounts don't cancel; they reorder less, then less
   often (monthly→quarterly), below their own seasonal pace, then go quiet. #1 early
   signal: gap/decline vs. the account's OWN reorder pattern (RFM recency/frequency).
   Standard mechanism: missing/off-pace-order alert + win-back cadence. **Highest-ROI
   first system for GT** — runs on existing Shopify data. *(high · repspark,
   wholesalehelper, prospectsoft [food&beverage]; 3-0 across four claims)*
8. **Lean acquisition = Dream 100** — hand-picked ~50–100 most-wanted accounts (chains
   weighted: one win = many branches), persistent varied touches + samples +
   education-based marketing. Not high-volume cold outreach. *(medium · Holmes/
   predictableprofits 7-step; single operationalization source, canonical framework)*
9. **Track 5–9 KPIs only**, chosen by "where would poor performance most jeopardize the
   business." GT set (synthesized): reorder recency/frequency per account · NRR ·
   % chain branches active · new accounts/mo · AOV+order count. Minimal stack = existing
   tools (Shopify + Make + lightweight layer), no new platform. *(medium · NetSuite +
   Miller 7±2; GT-specific list is synthesis, not source)*

## Refuted claims (do NOT rely on)

- ✗ "Avoid outbound below ~$10k deal size" — wrong denominator for recurring accounts;
  GT annual account value is 5×–200× the order (see evidence snapshot). (1-2)
- ✗ "KAM starts by announcing a program and selecting accounts before process/tooling." (0-3)
- ✗ "A motion is repeatable only after a 3–5 standard-deal test." (0-3)
